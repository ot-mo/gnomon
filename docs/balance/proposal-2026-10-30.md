# Balance proposal: fight cost, XP, bare hands

The analysis was run against `integration` @ `5ae6f7f`. Nothing in the repo was changed. The scripts are in this folder
(`lib.mjs`, `build.mjs`, `typical.mjs`, `final.mjs`, `xp.mjs`, `extras.mjs`, `species.mjs`, `risk.mjs`; grid results are
in `search*.json`). This is for your approval: no number changes until you say so.

## 0. In short

A typical build today (a fighter background, powers as offered, C4's gear as found) pays **2-7%** of its health per
same-level kill. The parent paid 9-35%. The typical build has about 1.7× the HP of the old harness fighter, twice its
armour (C4 added helms, bracers, gauntlets, boots and material AV), and kills twice as fast (Quick strikes, Mighty, a
higher STR).

To reach "like the parent", creatures must scale about three times as steeply. I propose **HP +18% a level** (now +6%)
and **damage +3, then +2 every 3 levels** (now +1 every 4). This applies under the d20 set only, so the parent's numbers
and golden stay as they are. Add two species fixes. The result is **9 / 10 / 10 / 18% at tiers 1-4** (parent: 9 / 9 /
10 / 16) and **23.5 / 27 / 30 / 38.5% at tiers 5-8** (parent: 25 / 30 / 35 / 25). Kills take 2.8-4.1 s at tiers 5-8, near
the parent's 3.0-4.5 s.

XP needs no change: exploring alone already carries you to about each tier's level. Bare hands need a slower punch.

## 1. How it was measured

- **Typical build.** Four fighter backgrounds: Aspirant of the Rule, Forgehand of Orrum, Drover's Child and Reedcutter,
  each with the wizard's suggested spread (`chargen.js suggestSpread`) and its background bonus. Each runs 20 seeds of
  `leveling.js offerPowers`. At every level the player takes a combat power if one is offered, in this order: Quick
  strikes, Mighty, Keen eye, Tough, Iron skin, Executioner, Regeneration, Crushing, and so on. Otherwise they take the
  first offer. Every 4th level's attribute point goes into STR, then AGI. No mutations and no implants (neither changes a
  melee kill here). By level 14 the Aspirant has taken about 11 combat powers in 13 picks; the Drover's Child about 8.
- **C4's gear.** The kit starts with the background's own kit. Then come `randomGear` draws at each band's level: 1
  weapon and 2 armour pieces in tier 1, then 2 weapons and 4 armour pieces a tier, all kept. The player wears the best of
  what they can **see**: the weapon with the most damage per blow at their STR, and each slot's piece with the most
  armour. The enchantment comes as drawn, cursed pieces included. Elements and properties are left off. So are trinkets
  with no armour of their own (see 3.3).
  Not "the median of 200 draws" (a dagger or staff with leather or a cloak, which no fighter keeps), and not "the
  tier's standard class" (it ignores C4's eight armour slots). Sensitivity to this choice is in §4.
- **Creatures.** The real world's band (`tests/checks/combat.js realBand`, C3's regions), exactly as the repo harness
  uses it.
- **Cost.** As in `ttkTables`: the creature's damage per second × your time to kill it ÷ your max HP. Melee only.
- **Model.** I computed the exact d20 outcome (every d20 roll, damage roll and soak, crits, Executioner below a quarter
  of HP, cursed armour's negative soak) and the expected number of blows to kill. This model reproduces the repo's d20
  and real columns within sampling noise (for example tier 5: 22.2% against 22.5%; tier 8: 51.6% against 52.6%). It
  also matches the game's own `resolveAttack`, sampled for 48 build-and-tier cases, within ±1 point. The exception is
  builds with Executioner (up to 3 points): the harness's sampling can never trigger it, because it gives the creature
  1e12 HP.

## 3. The proposal: exact edits

### 3.1 Creature level scaling (d20 set only; the legacy set keeps the parent's numbers, so golden holds)

| band level | 2 | 5 | 8 | 11 | 14 | 17 | 20 | 23 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HP × now (+6% a level) | 1.06 | 1.24 | 1.42 | 1.60 | 1.78 | 1.96 | 2.14 | 2.32 |
| HP × proposed (+18%) | 1.18 | 1.72 | 2.26 | 2.80 | 3.34 | 3.88 | 4.42 | 4.96 |
| damage + now (1 per 4) | 0 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| damage + proposed (3, then 2 per 3) | 3 | 5 | 7 | 9 | 11 | 13 | 15 | 17 |

1. **`src/rules.js:34`** (RULES.d20): `… reqPenaltyMax: 6, fistReq: 10 },` → `… reqPenaltyMax: 6, fistReq: 10,
   creatureHp: 0.18, creatureDmg: 3, creatureDmgEvery: 1.5 },`. Also add a comment line in the block above:
   "creatures (creatures.js spawnCreature, not bosses or guardians): health × (1 + creatureHp × (level − 1)), damage +
   creatureDmg + floor((level − 1) / creatureDmgEvery); the legacy set keeps the parent's +6% and +1 per 4".
2. **`src/creatures.js:40`**: add `import { RULES } from "./rules.js";`.
3. **`src/creatures.js:96`**:
   - old: `const hp = Math.round(t.hp * (t.boss || t.guardian ? 1 : 1 + 0.06 * (level - 1)) * (e?.hp || 1));`
   - new: `const D = RULES.combat === "legacy" ? null : RULES.d20, big = t.boss || t.guardian;` followed by
     `const hp = Math.round(t.hp * (big ? 1 : 1 + (D ? D.creatureHp : 0.06) * (level - 1)) * (e?.hp || 1));`
4. **`src/creatures.js:103`**:
   - old: `bonus: Math.floor((level - 1) / 4) + (e?.dmg || 0),`
   - new: `bonus: (D && !big ? D.creatureDmg + Math.floor((level - 1) / D.creatureDmgEvery) : Math.floor((level - 1) / 4)) + (e?.dmg || 0),`
   - Also update the comment at line 73. Mortal villagers (line 83) keep +6%.
5. I left **`RULES.d20` hitPerLevels 3 and dvPerLevels 4** as they are. Raising creature to-hit to 1 per 2 levels fit
   tiers 1-3 slightly better, but it is one more knob.

### 3.3 Gear enchantment (C4's material ladder: no change)

9. **`src/content/items/legacy.js:368`**: today every worn piece adds its enchantment to AV, even one with no armour, so
   a +3 monocle, cloak or map case is AV 3. In the found kits that adds about +1 AV at tiers 1-6 and +4 at tiers 7-8,
   which would cut tier 7-8 costs by about 4 points.
   - old: `av: (it) => ARMOURS[it.id].armour + it.ench + materialAv(it),`
   - new: `av: (it) => (ARMOURS[it.id].armour > 0 ? ARMOURS[it.id].armour + it.ench : 0) + materialAv(it),`
   - The parent's armour all has armour 1 or more, but rerun golden.
10. **MATERIALS** (dmg −1…+6, AV 0…+3) and **randomGear's** symmetric ±enchantment stay as they are. The scaling above
    is fitted to them. The repo harness's "+level/6 weapon, +level/8 armour" was its own stand-in for tier gear and
    should go when the harness moves to the typical build (3.6).

## 6. XP: "mostly exploring"

What the real world pays. These are counts from `buildRun` (seeds 1234 and 7); the code is the source, and differs from
the HANDOFF:
- Discovery: 16 landmarks (7,287 XP) and 33-36 other sites (ruins, lairs, stations, historic: 5,000-5,300), so about
  **12,300-12,600**, not the HANDOFF's 8,600 (that was before C1b).
- Villages and their quests: 16 villages, 4,400-5,000 XP for visits and 13,200-14,900 for quests.
- The campaign: 11 beats, 15,900 XP.
- Exploring all of it pays **46,000-48,000 XP**. Level 23 costs 38,170; level 25 costs 49,220.
- Triangulations would add up to 15,500-17,800 XP if every event's three accounts were found. `siteBottom` (0.4) is in
  the table, but nothing pays it.

Mean of the two seeds. The world is explored tier by tier. A good place to be at the end of tier T is level 3T + 1,
one below the next tier's surface level:

| level at the end of tier | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| target 3T + 1 | 4 | 7 | 10 | 13 | 16 | 19 | 22 | 25 |
| exploring only (all of it, no kills) | 4.0 | 6.7 | 10.8 | 14.9 | 17.5 | 21.1 | 23.6 | 24.7 |
| all + 25% of triangulations + 40 kills a tier | 4.2 | 7.3 | 11.5 | 15.7 | 18.4 | 22.3 | 25.1 | 26.3 |
| 75% of sites/villages/quests (+25% tri, 40 kills) | 4.0 | 7.0 | 11.0 | 14.9 | 17.6 | 21.3 | 24.1 | 25.4 |
| the same with the trim (3.5) | 4.0 | 6.9 | 10.7 | 14.5 | 17.0 | 20.6 | 23.4 | 24.6 |
| kills only (40 a tier, no exploring) | 2.4 | 3.9 | 5.4 | 6.9 | 8.4 | 9.9 | 11.4 | 13.0 |

Kills are already slow: 40 kills a tier add about 0.4 of a level, and kills alone reach level 13. **Exploring alone
keeps you at about each tier's level**, so no XP change is needed. A completionist runs 2-3 levels ahead mid-game. Under
the proposed scaling that is worth something: at +2 levels, tier 5-7 kills cost 19-27% instead of 23-30%. The optional
trim (3.5) takes about half a level off the mid-game. If `siteBottom` is ever wired, it adds about 3 levels; it would
then need its own trim.

## 7. Bare hands

FISTS today (0.3 s, the dagger's swing; damage 1-2 + (STR − 10) / 2) out-fight the weapons a typical fighter finds. At
STR 20 a punch does 6-7, about 22 a second; a greatsword does about 13 a second. Cost per kill at tiers 1-4, against
the kit's weapon:

| | T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| now: the kit's weapon | 3.3% 0.8 s | 2.8% 0.9 s | 1.7% 0.9 s | 3.5% 1.4 s |
| now: bare hands | 2.4% 0.6 s | 2.1% 0.7 s | 1.3% 0.7 s | 2.3% 0.9 s |
| proposed scaling: the kit's weapon | 9.4% 0.9 s | 9.7% 1.1 s | 9.5% 1.3 s | 17.7% 2.2 s |
| proposed scaling: bare hands as now | 6.7% 0.6 s | 6.7% 0.8 s | 7.1% 1.0 s | 12.1% 1.5 s |
| **… swing 0.5 s (proposed)** | **11.2% 1.1 s** | **11.2% 1.3 s** | **11.8% 1.6 s** | **20.2% 2.5 s** |
| … STR over 16 instead of 10 | 11.0% 1.0 s | 9.6% 1.1 s | 9.9% 1.3 s | 17.1% 2.1 s |
| … both | 18.3% 1.7 s | 15.9% 1.9 s | 16.5% 2.2 s | 28.6% 3.5 s |

A 0.5 s punch puts bare hands a little below a weapon, which is where they belong. Both changes together would be
punitive. Gauntlets (1-4) and the Forgehand's truesteel knuckles (2-8, crush) scale the same way and stay good.

## 8. What I'm unsure of

1. **The typical build is my construction.** The backgrounds spread widely (tier 8: Aspirant 31%, Drover's Child 51%).
   A player who never favours combat powers pays 27-57% at tiers 5-8 and still wins (1.9× at tier 8). The proposal
   makes powers and full armour matter. A plain build (no powers, body armour only) would lose from tier 5.
2. **The proposal is about 3× the parent's slope.** At level 23 the +17 damage outweighs a kind's own dice (3-11), so
   species differ more by HP and speed than by bite. Archers get the +17 on their shots too (`ai.js:295`), and so do
   followers.
3. **Light weapons.** STR over the requirement and the material add per blow, so a strong fighter's dagger or stylus
   kills about 2× faster than a greatsword. A player who picks by speed pays 12-21% at tiers 5-8 under the proposal. I
   did not fix this; one direction is to scale the STR term and the material by swing ÷ 0.5. Your call.
4. **The table is melee only, like the repo harness.** It leaves out venom, the trolls' regeneration, drain, ranged
   attacks, elites (up to 22% of spawns from level 18, more damage) and packs (cairn hounds come 3-5). Real fights cost
   more than the table says.
5. **Tiers 5 and 8 sit at the edges** (23.5% and 38.5%) because of their band mixes: stragglers at tier 5, only fresh
   Lattice kinds at tier 8. Fix 8 or the regions' level ranges would tune them.
6. **Gear upgrades matter less than levels.** A kit two tiers old costs 0-7 points more at tiers 5-8. Levels (+18% HP a
   level on creatures; powers, TOU and HP for you) carry most of the curve. If you want gear to weigh more, steepen the
   material ladder and the scaling together.
7. **Dungeon floors to level 30** get the same curve: ×6.2 HP and +22 damage at level 30.
8. **XP.** Triangulation (how many a player completes) and Kindling counts are guesses. The tier-by-tier route is an
   idealisation.
