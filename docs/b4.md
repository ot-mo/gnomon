The user's request (2026-10-31): requirements over several attributes, Elden Ring–style. The proposal and its numbers
are `docs/balance/requirements-2026-10-31.md`. Branch `ws/b4`, from `p4i-six`; tag `p4b-b4`. The gate's numbers and the
independent review are in `docs/sessions/b4.md`.

## The user's decisions (2026-10-31, in the coordinating session, relayed to B4)

- **"Then follow your plan for balancing"**, after the brief. The coordinating session ruled that B4's own
  recommendations are built without asking again.
- **The "used badly" rule: "no that sounds good".** It was put to the user as: you can still use gear you don't meet
  the requirements for, at a to-hit penalty, 60% damage and without its class power, as Elden Ring does it, or else it

- **A point every 3 levels: "yes"**, as a follow-up after the requirements build. It is in `p4b-b4` (2d387d6).

The integrator should record these in DECISIONS (the attribute set's requirements, the growth pace) and HANDOFF at the
merge. The bible's "+1 attribute point every 4 levels" (§10.7, §15.6) is overridden.

## What's in

Everything is under the attribute set only (`RULES.stats === "attributes"`). The legacy stat set and legacy combat
are the parent's (golden, the legacy fights and the screen frames untouched and green).

- **Tables** (`content/items/forge.js`): `REQUIRES` for every weapon, missile weapon and armoured piece, and
  `SCALING` with grades S-E for every weapon. `GRADES` holds each grade's share. `MATERIAL_REQ` holds the materials'
  shifts, as affinities shift weapons; they apply to every forged piece. Every starting kit fits its background at
  creation.
- **A weapon you lack the attributes for is used badly** (`combat.js`):
  - to hit −2 a missing point, at least 4, at most `reqPenaltyCap` 8 (the legacy set's cap stays `reqPenaltyMax` 6);
  - damage ×0.6, with nothing from your attributes;
  - its class's power off;
  - it keeps its own bonuses (properties, a relic's gifts).
- **A launcher you lack them for:** the same to-hit cost and ×0.6 (`missiles.js`).
- **Armour you lack them for** (`stats.js`): half its AV, −1 DV a piece, none of its own bonuses, and its block or
  reflection off (`combat.js turnedAside`).
- **A weapon in a hand that isn't the primary one** (never swung there) is held to its requirements as armour is.
- **Which pieces you meet** is a least fixed point, the same in any order: a piece's penalties always count, and its
  bonuses wait on its requirements. The bonus cache keys on `RULES.stats`.
- **Met, a blow adds** Σ grade × (attribute − 10) ÷ 2, scaled by its swing, in place of STR over 10. Fists keep STR
  over 10.
- **No lock** (`body.js canEquip`): under the attribute set the piece goes on, to be used badly. The legacy stat set
  still refuses.
- **Text:**
  - the item's line: "needs STR 21, AGI 17 · scales STR B, AGI D · you lack 3 STR: used badly (…)";
  - the pack row: "STR 21 AGI 17", each red when you lack it;
  - a red shortfall line under the pack's item pane (the box grows a line under the attribute set);
  - the log when you put on or draw such a piece.
- **An attribute point every 3rd level** (`leveling.js` `attrPointEvery` 3; the character sheet's line reads the rule).

## Requests (left for others)

1. **Artifact weapons' INT** (left out, as the proposal recommended): the glimgun INT 17 and the lancet INT 21, a shot
   ×0.6 when unmet, in `bodymods/artifacts.js`.
2. **Specials read outside the stat bus** stay on when a piece is worn badly: the hooks' climb, the pack's carry, the
   banner's rally, the veils. Only the stat-bus bonuses, the buckler's block and the glass-scale's reflection go.
   Turning the others off belongs to each special's owner.
3. **The pack's comparison** (`inventory.js compareLine`, "+N avg damage vs yours", B1 and C4 #12) still compares
   range + material. It doesn't count your grades, or ×0.6 for a weapon you can't use. Making it count them changes
   C4's held numbers in check-items' "compare" section, so it's left for whoever owns that line.
4. **The Dark backstab power** still doubles a blow from the dark with any weapon, one used badly included. It is your
   power, not the class's. If it should go with the class power, that is a one-word change in `combat.js`
   `gearMultipliers`.
