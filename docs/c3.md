## Integration requests

1. **Levels above the parent's 20** (`rules.js`, frozen; `creatures.js`). Raise `RULES.level.contentMax` from 20 to 23
   (bible §4.5: tiers 1-8, the surface to level 23), and in the same change let `creatures.js pickCreatureType(rand,
   level, table = null)` draw a level above 20 with no table from `content/creatures/levels.js LEVEL_POOL` through
   `pickRow` (one draw, as now; `?? "goblin"` stays the last resort). The parent's tables end at 20 (golden (b)), so
   with the clamp raised and nothing else every such draw falls through to the "goblin" default: makeLegend at a
   bottom + 3 above 20, a region's rows with nothing at a level, WS8's TTK tier 8 (level 23). What the raise changes
   besides: prices (`currency.js priceLevel`, up to × 2.53 at 23 from × 2.33) and the surface's rewards
   (`gen/biomes/common.js surfaceReward`) at levels 21-23. P0's `tests/checks/stats.js:111` (`contentLevel(25) === 20`)
   and `tests/checks/currency.js:80` (`priceLevel(30) === priceLevel(20)`) hold the 20 and change with it. I'd keep 23,
   not 30, until the balance phase has looked at prices above 20.
2. **The three registers** (`screens/examine.js`, WS5's; `screens/target.js lookLine`, WS8's). A creature's examine
   shows `creatureLines(c.type)` (`content/creatures/bestiary.js`) by how you look: the Flat line on the map, the Dark
   line with the torch out (in 3D or on the map), the Deep line otherwise; for the parent's flatling, shade and wraith
   too (their entries are golden, so their lines live only in the bestiary). Today examine shows `t.info`, the Deep line.
3. **Bands by region** (`overworld/encounters.js bandOf`, WS1's). Read `ENCOUNTERS.regions[tile.region].rows` with

   band is the parent's goblins and rats. One draw a member, as now, so the travel stream's count doesn't change.
4. **Legends by region** (`world/sites.js makeLegend`, WS10's). On a site whose region has `LEGENDS.regions[region]`
