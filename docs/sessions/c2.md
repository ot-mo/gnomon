## 4. Deviations, with reasons

1. **The history grammar is 2,864 words, over §15.9's 2,400** (+19%). It was 2,817 at `p3i-props`: WS7 wrote each of
   §8.3's 33 variants three accounts, most with a second wording, which keeps a run's accounts apart (WS7 review a3).
   C2 added §8.2's fourth relic power (47 words). Trimming the second wordings would bring it near 2,400 and reopen a3;
   left for the user. check-story 10 stops it growing.
2. **Faction greetings at Grey, Warm and Bright only;** Cold stays the role's curt line (it is about the role's manner,
   and check-social's assertions on it hold). Needs request 2 to show.
3. **`buys` can say only "these reading kinds and these topics"** (`ritual.js wants()`). Where the bible names a
   subject (the Vigil: readings about Noon; the Assize: Bent villages' Locations and Hour relics) `subjects` says it for
   the menu to read; until then they buy the whole topic. The Survey's "deep readings; Locations of ruins and Hour
   sites; Flat Accounts" is an AND here (flat or deep, location or history); the Unlit can't buy tomb Locations (a
   Location is a flat reading). **The Flatfolk buy deep readings (§6.2), though §3.4 says they buy Flat Accounts**: the
   bible disagrees with itself; C2 took the faction's own entry.
4. **`homeRegions`** held landmark letters nobody read; it now holds C1a's region ids, the letters moved to `homes`.
5. **C2's numbers where the bible gives none:** the dishes' buffs (a day, small), the shrines' rewards (a village tale,
   night eyes, a heal; once a day), the commons lamp's and the money's lines, the flasks' words, the cults' liquid
   (sunwine, the Horary's: "misguided fellow believers") and voice. The dark-pass is unpriced (the bible: "earn").
6. **Relic powers that aren't bonus keys are ability ids** (WS7's convention; Appendix A), none implemented (request 10).
7. **The shown words wait for the code:** a vial's drink words say what drinking does today and the bible's are in
   `full`; the village pillar's prayer is read by looking up (WS7b's 4-block column), the bible's climb in `full`.
8. **The Pressed's villagers use the Flatfolk's letter names** (§2.3 gives the Pressed no phonology); **the Unlit
   speak in lower case** (ghosts keep their names so, §2.3).
9. **The test world's villages draw differently** (names' list lengths move the draws after them: callings, a
   building, the villager count, the quest; 1,523 → 1,506 villagers); every real-world history differs after its first
   relic. Request 14 asks for `GEN_VERSION`'s bump.
10. **check-social's flame-kin menu test** (WS6b's) set the Drovers' `buys` aside for its default-rule part (request 15).
