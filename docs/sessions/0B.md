## 4. Deviations from the design, with reasons

1. **Attributes follow bible §10.6, not TECH_DESIGN §2.6**: max HP = TOU + 4 + (level − 1) × max(1, 3 + TOU mod), no
   `hpRolls`; prices follow §15.6; there is no `pv` key (DECISIONS: no penetration dice).
2. **Tough and Mighty write base fields** through `raiseMaxHp`/`raiseAttr`, not bonuses, so golden's `bonus(maxHp)` and
   `bonus(str)` stay exact. The Swimmer perk gives a `swim` bonus that `canSwim` reads.
3. **`RULES.regen.base = 60`** is a placeholder: neither document gives a number. `RULES` is a proxy rather than an
   `Object.freeze`d object, so `extendRules` can still add namespaces.
4. **Statuses**: the salamander's bite is `burning` at half rate (still 1 damage a second) under the attribute
   formulas; under the legacy set it is the parent's poison (verification fix `01f710d`); poison stops regeneration
   only under the attribute formulas (the legacy set keeps the old behaviour); in deep water drowning's ×0.4 multiplies
   the swim speed 0.45 in both views; statuses use `period` (seconds per point) instead of `dps`.
5. **Rings**: E in the inventory puts a ring on the first finger, so you wear one ring at a time as in the old game
   (bible §13.3); the second finger takes one only by part id. The humanoid's parts are ordered hands, body, fingers,
   neck, so bonuses add up in the old order. A two-handed weapon's second hand holds the primary hand's id.
6. **STR requirements still refuse** gear outright; turning that into a to-hit penalty is 0C's (d20). Every old item
   weighs 0 for now. Uses are named actions ("drink", "read", "wear") registered by `inventory.js` (direct imports
   caused import-order cycles).
7. **The purse cap applies to the old game too** (DECISIONS: 200 at the start). Deep down the parent's prices go past
   it (known issue 1); nothing that gives money is lost: a pile keeps the rest, a reward drops it at your feet, a sale
   is refused.
8. **`gold` stays a registered kind**: the same pile as `cash` (golden (a)'s scenario drops one, and the parent's levels
   name it). Generation lays `cash` only; the golden adapter shows `cash` under the parent's name for the dungeon and
   vault facts (only the `kind` string differs: without the renaming, 95/95 dungeons differ in `items` and 9 vault
   stamps in `items.N.kind`, nothing else).
9. **The map legend still says "gold"** next to `$`: golden (b)'s map frames include the legend, and "glim" there
   changes 9 of the 27 frame hashes. It becomes glim when golden (b) is retired or the legend becomes content.
10. **Torch drain is not built**: whether it is dark needs the clock (surface night), which is 0D2's `clock.js`;
    `skipTime` emits `time:skip` for it. Nothing in 0B drains glim.
11. **The bot's golden (a) replay** turns the scenario's steps into key presses; picking up a crystal lies it on the
    next tile on the map (a map step onto it), at your feet in 3D (a frame). On the map, using things takes game time,
    so statuses run out during the replay (47.9 s); the trace still matches, since it records nothing time-dependent.

## 5. Known issues

1. **Prices deep down outgrow the 200 purse**: a heart crystal costs 150 × (1 + level/15) (200 at level 5, 300 at
   15, ×1.5 after each one bought), forging an element 150 × the same, a bold shrine offering 80 + 12 × level (more than
   200 below level 10). Without a bigger purse flask (there is no item for it yet) these are out of reach in the
   legacy campaign. Prices and loot are C2's and WS6b's (bible §15.6: value × (1.5 − 0.1 EGO mod − step); loot 10 + 4 ×
   tier per floor).
2. `canPickUp` for piles reads `purseRoom`, the counter backing's; an item backing (WS6b) needs its own.
3. During `skipTime`, poison clouds only thin out (they don't poison anyone), and bombs and projectiles don't advance.
4. `world.js` `floatsOn` still checks the Swimmer perk directly rather than `bonus(swim)`.
5. Creatures fall back to `e.maxHp` and STR 10 until 0C; the 12% crit and haste multipliers are still in
   `monsters.js`/`player.js` (`RULES.legacy.crit`, `RULES.haste` exist for when 0C moves them).
