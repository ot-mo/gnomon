## 4. Deviations from the design, with reasons

1. **The HUD shows an ability only while it cools down** (nothing while ready): showing it always would change
   `p0-0c`'s recorded frames. The hotbar is invisible until the first use.
2. **Every player has Fold Step** (`innate`, hotbar key 1) in P0; WS4 turns it into the Bent's mutation and drops the
   grant. Keys 1-9 were free in both views. `requestTarget` looks 10 tiles out (`REQUEST_RANGE`).
3. **The clock's looks stay at noon** (`daylight` 1, the noon sun and sky, no fog) until WS3, so no frame changes.
4. **`game.time` stays a plain field** and `state.time` is its view (`attachState`): an accessor on the game object made
   every `game.time` access 10-100× slower.
5. **`seen` and `holeSeen` are deflated like the blocks**, not plain b64 as §7.2 says: about 5 KB → 40 bytes each.
6. **The creature whitelist also keeps** `state`, `phase`, `risen`, `bounty`, `lightBurn` and `regenAcc`: without them a load
   breaks Rook's quest and resets the Lich King's fight.
7. **The bot's session starts on legacy level 1** (the temple's own stairs are gated by its story); write-behind starts
   once a run has a slot; a game loaded into 3D opens on the pause card (a click takes the pointer); a load uses
   `game.world`, not `state.world.id` (one world is played in P0).
8. **No `AudioContext` on check pages** (`test-world.html`): the sound effects' `Math.random` jitter shifted
   `check-legacyfights`' 3D fights in the browser; without it browser runs match Node.
9. **Small edits outside the plan**: read-only registry getters (`events.busCounts`, `keys.boundCodes`,
   `talk.serviceIds`/`talkOptionIds`, `currency.backingKinds`, `quests.questIds`, `abilities.abilitySources`,
   `items.itemActions`, `interact.useIds`, `temple.TEMPLE_LEGENDS`) for the checks; `checkWorld` takes the bible's text;
   `tests/checks/save.js` loads `node:fs` lazily.
10. **0A2's signatures kept as built**: `heightAt`/`treeAt` don't match §1.4's; `BIOME_BUILDERS` lives in
    `gen/biomes/index.js`, re-exported from `gen/surface.js`. Left unfrozen where the design names nothing:
    `encounters`, `stations`, `reputation`, `builders`, `shrines`, `backings`, `journal`, `campaign`, `missiles`, the
    equipment screen's mode, several result and argument shapes.
11. **`check-names`' qualified entries** (§15.11 bans glimmer as a psychic measure, chimera as a morphotype, Wayfaring,
    Physic and Tinkering as skill names): flagged everywhere unless `ALLOWED` lists the use with a reason, since no
    scan can tell the sense; the old game's "shrinks to a glimmer" (a torch's light) is the only one. Classic, Roleplay
    and Wander follow DECISIONS: only next to "mode", plus `classic`/`roleplay` as identifiers ("a variable called
    classic is still renamed"); a creature's `wander` state is not a hit. Comments are scanned too. "Mutated Human" is
    added for R8's "the research notes' names for genotypes" (the notes' own list is all in §15.11). The bible's 10,000
    generated names (§15.8 item 12) wait for WS7's generators: until then the check scans every string of the Run data.
12. **`check-content`'s coverage rule** exempts the test world's legacy floor 1 by name: the parent's floor 1 spawns
    two types (rat, goblin), and changing that would change the legacy fights `check-legacyfights` holds. A "loot
    table" in P0 is the legacy one (gear of that level through `maxTierAt`, potions, scrolls); a region's own
    `creatures` or `loot` table is read when C1 adds one. Its 13 mutations run with every check, so its teeth are tested.
13. **`check-perf` is Node only** and tells the machine from the tree: when an item is over budget it measures
    `p0-0d1` alternately in the same conditions, and an item over budget in both and at most 15% slower is a note,
    not a failure (the outdoor frame here). Without a reference every item over budget fails. The dungeon frame,
    which §6.7 has no row for, is held to the outdoor 17 ms. `skipTime` is measured with no statuses (the bulk path);
    `check-status` holds the poisoned case. Not measured yet: the sun bake (WS3), the world map draw (WS1), `present`.
14. **`registerUse`'s legacy uses return `true`** (the map's usual 0.5 s, `turns.js` `USE_TIME`) rather than seconds, so
    the four uses act exactly as before; the map walks into a block with a use beyond the four (the four still by the
    tile's kind, so bumping a lit brazier stays silent); a third argument `{ label }` gives the crosshair's line in 3D.
15. **`browser-0d1`'s HUD window is 30 cells tall whatever the font**: Windows' Consolas makes 11-pixel cells, so its
    600×300 window was 100×27 here (and failed at `p0-0d1` too); it is now resized to 600 × 30 cells (330 px here).
    `browser-shell`'s small window stays 600×300 (100×27 here: every mode still passes).
16. **(§7) A new quick game starts with no glim**, as the parent started with no gold, so in the old campaign the torch
    gutters 240 s into the first dungeon unless you have picked up cash by then (level 1 lays a few piles; 3 on seed 99).
    The drain is DECISIONS' and the start purse is the old game's: a starting amount would change the HUD's `$0` in the
    recorded frames (golden (b), `check-screen-frames`). WS4's backgrounds give 60-250 glim (bible §3.2).
17. **(§7) The P0 sample is called Fold Step but keeps its own numbers**: range 4 (the bible's is 4 + level), landing only
    where you could walk (`strict`), on the map toward the nearest hostile in sight or the way you face (the bible's: any
    explored tile, and a chasm drops you). WS4 replaces the def with the Bent's mutation (bible §15) and drops the innate
    grant; the id `foldStep` and hotbar key 1 stay.
18. **(§7) Zone records carry `ids`** (the id counter when snapshotted), not in §7.2's ZoneRecord: a load takes the highest
    of the slot's and every record's, since write-behind goes on writing zones made after the slot was saved (§7.4).
    The creature whitelist also keeps a guardian's `g` and `xp`; the attack cooldown `cd` stays out, transient like the
    player's `attackCd` (§2.4).
