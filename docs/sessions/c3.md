## 3. Deviations from the design, with reasons

1. **Seven creatures wait** (`waiting.js`) rather than being registered: without their system they would be wrong in
   the world, not merely plain. The regions' rows (C1a's) draw any registered kind into the dungeons: a glowfin or an
   eel would walk the dry floors, the salt mirage (a being of 3D alone) would be fought on the map, and the three
   bosses have no fight without theirs. Each entry is complete and held to the same checks; `needs` says what.
2. **The flatling, the shade and the wraith keep the parent's entries** (bible §12: "existing"): their entries are
   golden, so they have no d20 numbers of their own (species term 0, as today) and their registers live only in the
   bestiary (request 2).
3. **Region tables wait on code for travel and legends** (requests 1, 3, 4): until then a real-world band is drawn from
   the parent's tables (goblins and rats), a real-world legend's species too, and a draw above level 20 reads the
   parent's level 20. The data is in place and checked; the dungeons under the real world's sites draw the new kinds
   already (C1a's rows).
4. **No region's encounter `kinds`**: a region's own weights override the global ones, which is what check-overworld's
   fast-travel test zeroes to keep a journey going; they were flavour, so they are out.
5. **Temperaments by species** where the bible's hook means a fight and the faction's would not: the barrow skeleton
   and the longshadow (the Unlit are neutral), the Plan's lens sentry, beat-spider and golems (the Plan is neutral), the
   feral glass crab (the Clatter are docile), the hangvine, foldmonkey and moot-walker, the lamp-hound and the Gloam
   Warden; the outrider the other way (neutral, so out only for the Bent, whom the Assize hates). The Pleatwood's
   creasebeetle, the sporeling and the draughtsman stay their faction's: the wood's, the Moot's and the Plan's peace.
6. **The Gloam Warden runs the Lampless Saint's hook, not the guardian's**: a `guardian: "warden"` type would carry the
   parent's story with it (its wake line names the Gloom Warden, its fall opens a gate and drops a parent's relic).
7. **The sporeling splits into a `sporelet`** (a 64th type, "small sporeling"), as the parent's slime into its small
   slime, so a lilac sporeling doesn't split into green legacy slimes. Not a bible creature of its own.
8. **The people talk under their own ids** with WS6a's role conversations, registered from `people.js` (talk.js
   dispatches on the type's id; check-talk wants every person to have one). C5's words replace them.
9. **Corner, the temple cat, is `@`**: bible §15.7 gives talkable people `@` in their colour, and you pet her by talking.
10. **The encounter rows hold only what is out for a stranger**: WS1's band is "a hostile band"; a docile longback or a
    neutral kite would be met 8-12 tiles away and do nothing. So the Pleatwood's bands are the wood's other animals.
11. **The glowfin's "none (docile)"** is the beasts' hidden group with a docile temperament (no faction at all is
    hostile, factions.js); it waits anyway.
