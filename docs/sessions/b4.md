gear), check-golden (0 mismatched), check-legacyfights (0 differing) and check-stats, and wrote probe scripts. Its
findings, and what became of each:

1. **The legacy stat set under d20 had two of B4's paths: fixed** (46fb0b2, b3772fc).
   - (a) turnedAside's requirement test read the parent's STR, so a buckler or glass-scale below it stopped turning
     blows.
   - (b) `reqPenaltyMax` 6 → 8 reached the legacy to-hit path.

   Now `unmetOf` finds nothing under the legacy set, the parent's cap is 6, and B4's is `reqPenaltyCap` 8. My first
   assertion compared against the constant itself, so its mutant survived; b3772fc holds it to the literal 6.

   weapon is held to its requirements as armour is, and the log says so.

   to-hit, damage and class power only. Documented. The Dark backstab power with an unmet weapon is a request in
   `docs/b4.md`.
4. **A negative attribute bonus could make the fixed point disagree with attr() and depend on the order: fixed**
   (cffbede). Penalties always count.
5. **The bonus cache didn't follow `RULES.stats`: fixed** (a5c1ace). stats.js `bonusTable` keys on it.
6. **The pack's comparison ignores grades and ×0.6: left as a request.** Changing it moves C4's held numbers.
7. **The materials shift every forged piece, beyond §2's armour list: documented** in the proposal's §2.
8. **The Daze case passed on a one-point margin: fixed** (5f01e51). It now asserts the blow lands.
