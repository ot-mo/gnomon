### The carried-forward frame-time items

1. **The 0C camera-pop flakiness (seed 31) is not a frame-time regression.** Same-browser draw and tick times of p0-0b,
   p0-0c and HEAD are level (above). The cause: on seed 31 the route along the temple's east road arrives in `1,0,0`
   within `prefetchNear` tiles of its south edge, so `prefetchNeighbours` made `1,1,0` (12-38 ms) in the swap's own
   frame; the swap frame took 70-220 ms, its dt hit the 0.05 s cap, and the move was 3× the median per-frame move,
   which the old test called a pop. Generation cost is the same at p0-0b and p0-0c (12-27 ms per zone measured in the
   same browser), so p0-0c failing more often (5/10 vs 1/9) is the browser's jitter on a test that compared distance
   per frame.
2. **The pop test judges speed** (`f89222f`): each frame's dt is the change in `game.uiTime` between presents; the swap
   frame's speed must be ≤ 1.5× the typical speed + 0.5 tiles/s. Every run since: 4.2 tiles/s at the swap, typical 4.2
   (also on a 0.05 s frame: 0.21 tiles in 0.05 s). All 28 seed runs since (seeds 1234, 7 and 31) pass.
3. **Edge-swap and prefetch gaps** (headless Chromium, no GPU; median frame gap 17-26 ms): before, the swap frame
   46-87 ms on seeds 1234 and 7 and 70-220 ms on seed 31 (swap and prefetch together); one prefetch frame 12-38 ms of
   generation. After `d4a7156`: the swap frame no longer prefetches (seed 31's swap 31-98 ms), a surface zone is made in
   8 stages of 0-8.5 ms (the first three on seed 1234: 2.8, 3.3, 0.4 ms; the last 3.3 ms; was 21.5 ms in one), the frame
   finishing a prefetch 36-50 ms. (Verification, §7: this helps seed 31's case only; on seeds 1234 and 7 the swap
   frame is unchanged within noise, 40-79 ms at HEAD and 50-90 ms at p0-0c, once 113-124 ms.) What is left at the swap is `enterZone`'s apron rebuild (2-7 ms) and the
   first 3D draw in the new zone (16-56 ms against 13-32 for the next one): the renderer's, the same at p0-0b.

## 4. Deviations from the design, with reasons

1. **The transition screen's mode id is `transition`**, not the old `descend` (the §4.2 name).
2. **Pause is reached by losing the pointer lock** (Esc under the lock, or leaving the window), not by a key or click;
   rotate, transition, dead and won by game events. The gate's "every mode by keys and mouse" is read as: entered as
   play enters it, then used and left by keys and by clicks.
3. **I, J and Tab still work from the pause card**, as they did from the parent's paused view.
4. **M and N need no `when`**: talk and the shop take every key themselves (the first step's `when` is gone).
5. **At 100 columns the map header uses a shorter wording** so its clickable keys fit; at 180 it is the parent's. The
   short form keeps "T: throw" and "move/talk" ("bump to talk" before the cube); after the cube it leaves out
   "Space: wait" (still bound, and in the key help).
6. **HUD**: "AV/DV" shows `ARM n` under legacy rules and `AV n DV n` under d20; keys has priority 45 (§4.5 doesn't list
   it); with two rows, statuses may wrap onto row 1, and fps sits at the right end of the first row with room.
7. **Toasts**: any zone change without the DEPTH screen or the intro toasts (edge, travel, load): `TOAST_SILENT` is
   down, up, fall and start. The `inspect` payload names a creature only if it is still on that tile.
8. **Prefetch** (beyond §2.2's "at most one zone per frame"): never in a frame that swapped zones, and a surface zone is
   made in stages, one per frame (`ZONE_STAGED.surface`); dungeon levels have no staged generator (prefetch only makes
   surface neighbours). The map keeps making every open neighbour at once (time stands still between actions).
9. **`drawStack`** draws from the topmost full-screen (`over: "none"`) screen up; `dead` and `won` draw the view
   themselves under their card.
10. **The frame-time gate** compares in one page holding both trees, the trees alternating tick by tick (ABBA), on the
    ratio of the pooled trimmed means, rather than two separate runs: two pages swung the ratio ±6% between runs (a
    page behind the other runs slower), a fresh run per round ±50%. (Until the verification it used the median of
    40-tick blocks' ratios, which passed a real +6.5%: see §7.)

## 5. Known issues

- Headless Chromium has no GPU: the first 3D frame in a new zone costs 1.5-3× a normal one, and gaps of 100-240 ms
  happen anywhere (frames 1-15 after load, garbage collection). The swap frame is 31-98 ms there. Not measured in a
  GPU-backed browser in this session (none here); do it locally.
- On the map underground HEAD's tick is about 2% over p0-0c's (1 ms frames: the HUD layout, `beginFrame`, the overlay
  loop and `hoverInspect`, ~20 µs). Within the gate; worth a look if the map gets heavier.
- `game.prefetchJob` holds a generator: 0D2's save codec must skip it and `prefetchSeen` (both rebuilt on load).
- The 100×30 status line with seven statuses drops level, STR, AV/DV, funds, keys, torch and ready: by design
  (priorities; since the verification nothing of a lower priority fills a gap a higher one left), and WS4's ability
  cooldowns (priority 70) will push more off.
- From earlier sessions, still open: the torch drain waits for 0D2's clock; the map legend still says "gold"; the
  6-row horizon band outdoors (WS3); the Hollow Mere compiles as the Glass Sea (C1); thrown things stop at a zone's
  border; prefetch is skipped while `ZONE_LOADER.load` is set (WS9 prefetches through the loader).
