## 4. Deviations from the design, with reasons

1. **P0's check `tests/checks/world.js`, one rule** (the coordinator's approval, 2026-10-27; `6c65fae`, `// WS10`):
   its region-chance line is skipped for kind `"historic"`, which goes by tier band and regionPrefs; instead a
   historic site matches exactly one site request and sits on the requested tier or the nearest a usable span has,
   and each request is placed once. Each assertion fails on a mutant of `sites.js`: an off-tier placement ("on a
   tier-3 span, asked for tier 2"), a site with no request ("matches 0 site requests"), an unplaced request ("placed 0
   times").
2. **History's sites are placed after P0's kinds**, not first as bible §4.10 orders: a span made busy skips its site's
   draws, so placing them first moved every later village, ruin and lair (and put a ruin on one of `check-perf`'s
   sampled spans: surface p95 ×1.96). Each still gets a free span of its tier.
3. **A spike kill is nobody's** (WS6a request 2, carried to WS10): no reputation and no grudge, and also no
   experience and no kill effects of yours (vampirism's heal, a cursed ring's hurt), where the parent gave them;
   request 10 in `docs/ws10.md` asks the integrator to decide.
4. **Legends follow bible §12.13** (+3 levels) rather than TECH_DESIGN's "×1.5 level"; the bible outranks it.
   Five of the eleven templates are built (plain, Twice-pressed, Lampless Saint, Beatkeeper, Herdmother); the rest
   need systems not yet in (request 8). The +1 to attributes applies to creatures that carry attributes (none yet).
5. **`run.legends` stays empty in this tree**: the frozen `run.js` resets it after `placeSites`; the defs live on
   their lair sites and every reader looks there too (request 2).
6. **True-marks and body mods at the bottoms are seams** until WS5's merge (`BOTTOM_LOOT`, requests 3 and 4): the
   reliquary notes its true-marks and lays them once the `credit` kind exists; a lair's guaranteed loot is a
   treasure until `randomBodyMod` is wired.
7. **`holesIn` keeps random holes inside the rect**; a vault's own chasms (the Flat Lie, the Unseen Path) are its
   puzzle and stay, and such vaults never go on a last level.
8. **Station sites** are placed by a region's `sites.station` chance, which no fixture region has: the check builds a
   test world with one.
