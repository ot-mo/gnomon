## 3. Deviations from the bible, with reasons

1. **Waiting tables.** Mutations and artifacts that need code are data in `MUTATIONS_WAITING` and `ARTIFACTS_WAITING`,
   not in the live tables with a `needs` (as powers are): `mutationOffers` and `randomBodyMod` read every live entry, so
   a waiting one would cost 4 creases or lie in a ruin and do nothing. Each moves across when built (requests 7, 8).
2. **Material by `made`, damage by `material`.** combat.js and missiles.js already add an item's numeric `material`;
   the material's id goes in `made` (its name, price, AV, colour). makeGear sets both.
3. **A material's armour only on forged pieces** (body metal and the plain helm), not bracers, gauntlets, boots or
   the copper cap and iron helm (already their metal): a tier-8 body tops out near AV 10, not 20.
4. **The new classes have tier 0**: the parent's loot draws tiers 1-4 from WEAPONS (golden). The real world's loot is
   `randomGear`, which the generators don't call yet (request 1): until then C4's gear reaches the game through the
   kits only.
5. **Switched-on artifacts charge by the minute**: the featherfall lantern 1,200 (the bible's 20 a second) and falls
   don't hurt rather than a slow fall; jump-springs 100 (about 4 of the bible's 25-a-jump uses); the mirror shield 300
   (5 a second) with missiles 3 less instead of 50% reflected.
6. **Tonics**: everyone's second tonic in a row asks the TOU save (§13.8), the Bent get half and a 5% bad reaction
   (§10.3); "half" is half of every number (the balm 6, ironbark +1/+1, hasp 7.5 s). The balm's overdose is "no
   regeneration" (status flag noRegen) for 30 s: a healing potion still heals. Gloam gives 4 tiles until torchRange
   reads a status's dark range (request 10); clearwit's cure for confusion waits for a confused status.
7. **Marker chalk** marks the tile you stand on, from either view (markers already show in 3D; the chalk adds marking
   from 3D), a stick a mark, at most 9 a floor (the map's rule).
8. **Kits**: the ink pots are vials of ink; the stylus is a short blade (1-3); the examining kit is worn on the hands
   (+2 examine), the map case on the back (navigation 0.03: 3 points off the lost chance); the Aspirant carries the writ (worn, it would
   add +100 Assize to the bible's start); every kit holds a weapon in hand (WS4's deviation 1: bone or copper daggers).
9. **The staff is one-handed** (a walking staff): makeCharacter puts a kit's weapon straight on the hand, and a
   two-handed one would leave the other hand free (request 13).
10. **Not built**: the pack (back, +carry: there's no encumbrance), thrown discs (throwing.js: request in powers'
    Return), a melee knife (the throwing knife is the parent's). The jerkin is the parent's leather armour.
11. **Both the feathered hood (§13.3) and the feather cloak (§3.5)** give Kites +250 once the worn hook is in; they add up
    unless the hook caps a faction (request 2).
12. **The lamp crown** is both a worn head piece (§13.3) and the implant (§13.6), named the same, as the bible names them.
13. **The new armour pieces' tiers** are C4's (the bible gives none): where `randomGear` first lays them.
14. **The Pin power** overlaps the bolt-bow's own 2 s pin (the bible has both): left for the balance pass.
15. **Tonics, after the verification**: the chain lasts exactly the tonic's time or its overdose's (balm 30, hasp 18,
    gloam 20, ironbark 20, fold-sap 60, clearwit 1); a passed save ends the one before (its status: hasp's haste,
    fold-sap's flat too). The Bent's halves round down (clearwit +2 examine, +1 INT). The balm's overdose stops rest and
    balms for 30 s (a potion still heals); Gloam's is the torch shrunk to a flicker for 20 s (`darkened`): the bible's
    blindness and nausea wait for code (request 10).
16. **The forged helm** is the "close helm" ("saltsteel close helm"), so it is never named as the parent's iron helm.
17. **Exclusions none of the bible's**: Heavy Bones with Gills, Wings and Brittle Bones; Foresight with Beat-deaf (as
    WS4's deviation 27 for the live ones).
18. **The Assize writ is worn where an amulet hangs** (the neck): a document pinned there; it competes with amulets.
19. **Examine's description line on the parent's gear too** (verification A5): the bible's names read the same in both
    worlds; examine is WS5's screen, after the parent's game, and no fixture records it. Everything else of the
    parent's gear (name, line, price, glyph, palette, bonuses, AV) is frozen by `PARENT_GEAR`.
20. **The budget**: the area is 2,119 words of C4's files at the first count (2,240 now, check-content's way, with the
    pack and the texts from above), 12-13 an entry over about 180 entries; with C2's liquids about 2,300-2,400 against
    "about 2,000". §15.9's 160 entries counted fewer gear pieces than §13 lists; trimming WS5's implant lines (about 15
    words each) would win back 80 if the user wants it.
21. **The pack** (+30 carry) waits as a piece: carrying isn't limited (RULES.encumbrance is off).
