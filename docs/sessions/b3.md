## 4. Deviations, with reasons

1. **A P0 file changed, with approval: `src/torch.js`** (C2 #13; the coordinator's ninth exception, 2026-10-30, asked
   with the exact change first). Its own commit `d2267e8` with the `// B3 (C2 #13)` marker: the first time a run's torch
   gutters on an empty purse it says `CURRENCY.lines.empty`, later times the old line. The mark is
   `game.state.purseDryTold`, plain JSON in the saved state, so a save keeps it and a reload doesn't say the first line
   again (check-social 18 holds it through a JSON round trip; mutants `13-torch` and `13-torchOnce` caught).
   check-imports doesn't freeze torch.js's imports (it imports `CURRENCY` from `currency.js`, which it already imported);
   no fixture or recorded frame holds the line; check-torch PASS.
2. **GEN_VERSION is not bumped here**: B3 changes both worlds' runs (the cults in `run.factions`) and every village's
   surface (the heights). B1 and B2 may change generation too, so the bump is the integrator's, once for all three
   (`docs/b3.md` request 2).
3. **The heights are a 24 × 16 footprint's** (C2 #7 said the builder may need a bigger one): three levels for the ledges
   (0, 3, 6) and two pods for the canopy (6, 8) rather than "3-4 levels" and every house in a pod; the hall stays on the
   green in every raised style, so the leader (the quests' giver) is reachable on the map; the market at the cliff's top
   is the trader's house. Not stamped: the boardwalks over brine, the barrow's crypt, the bridges that fold at dusk, the
   Stacks' shaft, the glass walls seen through (the renderer's). In 3D a step of the stairs is a hop (Space), as the
   parent's crates are; the map climbs them one block a step.
4. **A Herdmother is talked to by L, not by talk**: a beast with a talk record would draw and stand as a person (P0's
   creatures.js), so the Saltbeasts' Herdmother's Lighting is done by looking at her (L) where it is ready. Her kindling
   waives the −250 floor (the Saltbeasts start at −400, so she could never be kindled otherwise; bible §12.13 means her
   to be).
5. **The dark-pass is earned at Bright** with the Lampless, for a Plumb (bible §3.3 says only "earned"), or given by
   content through `game.state.darkPass` (the Unlit Choir's plan says it comes from Halfnight: C5's to give).
6. **The Snuff and the fungal limb are not offered yet**: the Snuff mutation waits in `MUTATIONS_WAITING` (its code),
   and C4 made no fungal limb; each special turns on when its thing exists (`docs/b3.md` request 3).
7. **Turnwater's implant shutdown takes the implant's bonuses only** (its ability and its effect flag are WS5's
   `implants.js`'s readers: request 4); **confused** drifts your heading in 3D only (the map's steps are `turns.js`'s:
   request 5); **ink's shortcut** for flat beings and **umbra's hole** showing runes are not built (requests 5, 6).
8. **A dropped vial is poured**, not laid down: there is no other pour in the pack's keys, and an empty vial stays
   with you.
9. **Gossip is a rumour unasked on a village's first visit**: the talk option already gave rumours for nothing, so the
   power's "one free rumour in every village" had nothing to add otherwise.
10. **The commons lamp is the lamplighter's talk option** (and both views reach it: a bump on the map, E in 3D), not E
    on the lamp's block: a use on `B.LAMP` would put "use it" on every wall lamp of the parent's dungeons.
11. **The Chime** is EGO against WIL (d20 + EGO modifier ≥ 10 + WIL modifier), one chimed follower at a time, a sermon a
    creature a world day; it goes on a hotbar key by B. **Brew** is a vial of a base liquid and 10 glim at a village's
    oven, once a world day there (`BREWS`: water balm, sunwine hasp, umbra gloam, sap ironbark, ink fold-sap, clear glim
    clearwit). Neither is priced by the bible.
13. **The Ascent's 8 blocks are counted from the datum** (FLOOR_Z), as DECISIONS "Heights" counts every height; C2 #3
    says "above the zone's ground at your feet", which on a 12-high mesa top (the Tall Table, the Kites' own) would
    never be 8. Read so, the Kites' own aerie is where the Ascent is done.
12. **§7.7's Hour-stele hooks** (`HOUR_CULT.pray`, `desecrate`), **the Vigil's and the Pleatwood's trespass** and the
    **Hour mask** are not built (no data for the blessings; nothing in the game chips a block, touches the altar or cuts
    a branch; the mask is C2 #12's worn reputation): `docs/b3.md` request 9.
