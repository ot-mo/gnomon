Rules, in order: a landmark span is in its terrain's region (the Drowned Lantern in `glassSea`); a road span in its
road's region (a span on two roads: the first in `worldmap.js` ROADS, so (27, 11) and (36, 11) are `oldRule`); a river
span in its river's; the Old Cuts' two spans are `oldCuts`; any other land span with a Glass Sea span among its 8
neighbours is `glassShore`; everything else is its terrain's region (`&` by x, `~` by x). The terrain ids stay the
bible's, with one addition: the Mere's three spans get a terrain of their own, `mere` (`~` with x ≤ 6, split by x as
`&` is), because the code takes a span's name, colour and edge message from its terrain, and §15.2 already gives the
Mere its own tier (2) and palette in the sea's row. `sea` is the Glass Sea's alone (44 spans, tiers 5-7).
